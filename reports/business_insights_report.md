# Executive Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations Report

**Project Title**: E-Commerce Sales Performance & Customer Analytics  
**Timeline Analyzed**: January 02, 2026 – February 21, 2026  
**Total Net Revenue Analyzed**: ₹14,38,655 (~₹14.4 Lakhs) | **Total Orders**: 48 | **Average Order Value (AOV)**: ₹29,972  
**Audience**: Leadership, Commercial Strategy, and Sales Operations  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This study analyzed 50 raw transactions (48 validated after data hygiene checks) across 4 major geographic territories (North, South, East, West) and 3 merchandise categories (Electronics, Furniture, Clothing). Through relational SQL querying, customer RFM segmentation, and visual analytics in Tableau and Excel, we identified key growth levers, category dependency risks, and uncollected revenue opportunities.

### Headline Findings:
1. **Extreme Category Concentration in Electronics**:  
   Electronics generated **₹11,59,150 (80.6%)** of total company turnover across three hero products: Laptops (₹5.39L), Phones (₹3.70L), and Tablets (₹2.50L). Furniture accounted for **10.7%** (₹1.54L) and Clothing for **8.7%** (₹1.26L).
2. **High Customer Value Concentration**:  
   The top 3 customers—Aarav (₹5.39 Lakhs), Isha (₹3.33 Lakhs), and Sanya (₹2.50 Lakhs)—contributed **78.0% (₹11.22 Lakhs)** of total company revenue. Aarav alone generated 37.5% of total sales through repeat Laptop orders in the North region.
3. **100% Repeat Customer Retention**:  
   All 8 transacting customers placed between 5 and 7 repeat orders within the 2-month window, demonstrating exceptional customer loyalty and product-market fit.
4. **Regional Revenue Leadership in North & South**:  
   The **North** led regional turnover at **₹6,22,725 (43.3%)**, followed by the **South** at **₹4,00,610 (27.8%)**, **East** at **₹2,86,950 (19.9%)**, and **West** at **₹1,28,370 (8.9%)**.
5. **Immediate Cash Flow Recovery Opportunity**:  
   Tracking payment statuses revealed **₹50,220 in uncollected revenue** across 2 Pending orders (Meera ₹10,000, Arjun ₹25,100) and 1 Failed order (Diya ₹15,120).

## 2. Strategic Recommendations

| Priority | Action Area | Recommendation | Projected Commercial Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** | **Payment Recovery** | Implement automated SMS/WhatsApp recovery workflows for checkout drop-offs and failed payments. | Immediate recovery of **>₹50,000** in cash flow. |
| **High** | **VIP Key Account Management** | Establish dedicated loyalty perks and concierge service for top spenders (Aarav, Isha, Sanya). | Protects **78%** of core business revenue stream. |
| **Medium** | **Cross-Selling & Bundling** | Bundle high-margin accessories (Headphones, Shoes) with Laptop and Phone purchases at checkout. | Increases basket size and Furniture/Clothing AOV. |
| **Medium** | **West Region Expansion** | Investigate lower sales in West (₹1.28L vs ₹6.23L North); test localized promotions for Chairs and Shoes. | Diversifies geographic revenue dependency. |
